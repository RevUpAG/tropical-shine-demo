The right method

Soft wash vs. pressure wash

Not every surface should be blasted with high pressure. We match the method to the surface so it gets clean without getting damaged.

Soft wash Safer

Best for roofs, stucco, siding and painted surfaces

  • Low pressure — about like a garden hose — so it won't crack tiles, strip paint or force water behind walls.
  • Cleaning solutions kill mold, algae and mildew at the root instead of just knocking off the surface layer.
  • Because the growth is treated, surfaces tend to stay clean longer.

Pressure wash Powerful

Best for concrete, pavers, driveways and sidewalks

  • High-pressure water removes ground-in dirt, stains and tire marks from hard surfaces.
  • Ideal for durable surfaces that can handle the force.
  • Too much pressure on roofs or stucco can cause damage — that's where soft wash comes in.
